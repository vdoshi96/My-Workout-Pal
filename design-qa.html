Accessibility, interaction, and resilience

Every companion uses empty alternative text, aria-hidden="true", no accessible name or focus target, disabled dragging, and a pointer-inert slot. Strict protected-selector checks require both participants and prove non-overlap with fields, filters, reviews, records, guidance, timers, logging controls, notices, and navigation. Keyboard traversal, accessibility snapshots, pointer hit-testing, and automated accessibility checks confirm the art cannot consume or label an interaction.

Image failure and forced colors remove the complete slot. Reduced motion leaves static art unchanged. Authenticated fixture staging validates sources before locking, stages only the maintained asset manifest, records hashes in the lock, preserves pre-existing destinations, cleans partial copies, and gives each planned copy a unique hidden staging inode. Dead-owner recovery requires the expected hash, approved staging name and directory, and identical destination device/inode before deletion. Same-hash pre-existing destinations therefore remain untouched. Active owners still block concurrent staging.

Cancellation allowances remain restricted to allowlisted same-origin GET resources, the exact cancellation class, and bounded frame or superseding navigation evidence. Inherited helpers were not broadened.
