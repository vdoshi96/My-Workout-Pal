## Accessibility, interaction, and resilience

Every companion uses empty alternative text, `aria-hidden="true"`, no
accessible name or focus target, disabled dragging, and a pointer-inert slot.
Strict protected-selector checks require both participants and prove
non-overlap with fields, filters, reviews, records, guidance, timers, logging
controls, notices, and navigation. Keyboard traversal, accessibility snapshots,
pointer hit-testing, and automated accessibility checks confirm the art cannot
consume or label an interaction.

Image failure and forced colors remove the complete slot. Reduced motion leaves
static art unchanged. Authenticated fixture staging validates sources before
locking, stages only the maintained asset manifest, records hashes in the lock,
preserves pre-existing destinations, cleans partial copies, and gives each
planned copy a unique hidden staging inode. Dead-owner recovery requires the
expected hash, approved staging name and directory, and identical destination
device/inode before deletion. Same-hash pre-existing destinations therefore
remain untouched. Active owners still block concurrent staging.

Cancellation allowances remain restricted to allowlisted same-origin `GET`
resources, the exact cancellation class, and bounded frame or superseding
navigation evidence. Inherited helpers were not broadened.

   output described pre-existing CSS and scale advisories, not a new candidate

   dead-owner fixture cleanup, and board-sidecar provenance gaps.

   A subsequent complete WebKit-phone replay completed 21/4/1 because the

   immediately preceding product-corrected candidate passed the exact 22/4
   project before only the fixture-ownership hardening changed. The Wave 3
   source's teardown-only `/contours.svg` cancellation did not recur, and no
   exemption or timeout was broadened.
6. The fresh finish reviewer returned `fix` because the first stale-lock record
   could not distinguish a same-hash pre-existing destination from a copied

   focused staging passed 4/4, full Vitest passed 127 files/862 tests, and the
   independent re-review returned `ship` with no further material finding.
7. After the final browser replays, the reviewer classified the isolated fox
   cancellation and navigation timeout as engine-only evidence limitations;
   the `ship` disposition remained unchanged because both exact tests passed
   unchanged and no product assertion or policy changed.
