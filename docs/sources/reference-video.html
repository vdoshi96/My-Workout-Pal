Reference video notes

Publication boundary

This document summarizes product intent without reproducing the private recording, extracted transcript, audio, frames, thumbnails, or identifying prototype assets.

Observed flow

The recording opens on a mobile workout overview with several day tabs. A selected day presents a summary and sections for warm-up, main exercises, accessory work, core work, and cooldown. Each prescription exposes equipment, set and repetition targets, and prior result context.

Opening an exercise presents an embedded instructional video, rest interval choices, weight and repetition entry, set completion, and previous or next exercise navigation. A logged-set list appears after completion. Additional tabs expose strength summaries and form guidance. Separate navigation reaches an exercise directory and aggregate analytics.

Narrated intent

The narrator wants a personal routine split across training and climbing-related days. They emphasize seeing the structure of each day, opening YouTube guidance, logging sets and repetitions, and reviewing strength progress. They identify the displayed data as seeded and deletable and describe the prototype as requiring cleanup.

Product interpretation

My Workout Pal keeps the direct route from daily prescription to active logging and progress review. It replaces the reference routine with the user-specified five-day program and adds equipment confirmation, authenticated ownership, immutable history, interruption recovery, approved dual demonstrations, cardio logging, privacy controls, and explicit guest/sample labeling.
