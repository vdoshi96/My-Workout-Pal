2. What a first-time visitor sees now

  • Landing. The illustrated studio fills the opening screen. The heading is "Your new gym buddy." The four pillars (set your routine, track every set, watch a quick demo, see your gains) follow straight after. The page has no white cards.

  • Test drive (/try). A two-movement guest workout with approved demos: plan, demo in place, log a set, rest, finish, and a peek at how Progress adds up. Nothing is saved, and the page says so once.

  • Member onboarding. Five steps:

    1. What are you training for?
    2. How much lifting have you done?
    3. How many days a week can you train?
    4. What do you have to work with?
    5. Here's your routine.

    The routine is built from the answers by generateStarterRoutine and lists demos that open in place. "Prefer a different start?" offers the five-day example or a blank start. After saving, a four-card tour (Today, logging, demos, progress) can be skipped at any point. Skipping or finishing it moves focus to Today's heading. Unverified members can answer and preview but not save.

  • Today. The greeting names the next day ("Hey Alice! Ready for Push?"), followed by one big Start button, the week as day pills, the day's movements with in-place demos, and the progress glance. The chosen day is kept in the address.

  • Finishing a workout. A celebration ("Workout done! Nice work.") with a "New record!" ribbon when one was set. Focus moves to the heading.

  • Dark mode. Every scene has a dusk recolour, and the scene is masked into the indigo night canvas instead of dimming daylight art. The owner asked for this.

3. Navigation contract

Every drill-down shows a labelled back control that returns to the exact origin. Links carry a validated from, resolveBackTarget accepts only allowlisted origins, and BackLink steps back in history when the previous entry is the target, so scroll comes back. Demos open in a native <dialog> that mounts the video only while open.

Before (from the Phase 0 audit)

Back controls were forward links with fixed labels. Today's day picker reset on every round trip, guides always said "Library", Library search was lost, history detail always went to History, records lost their place, and the runner's demo hid in a collapsed disclosure.   • Two text pairs failed: white on a pale highlight in dark mode, 1.34:1.

After:

  • Text failures: none in any state.
  • Selected states: they all use one token set (--selected-bg/fg/edge), checked by tests/unit/design-token-contrast.test.ts in both themes.

  • The forced-colours copy-ratio check (above 0.8, one column).
