# Historical Wave 3 animal surface-system rollout brief

## Authority

This brief preserves the earlier Corner Companions rollout and its review questions as history. It does not govern Quiet Set. Root `DESIGN.md` and `docs/plans/QUIET-SET-IMPLEMENTATION.md` define the cream/forest system, original cartoon Pip/Mica, browser-local Off choice, and actual host eligibility. The former per-surface animals, transparent exports, placement matrix, and cache inventory below are historical.

## Scope and mode

The primary target is the authenticated Library at `src/app/app/library/page.tsx`.
Related targets cover the public Library, owned routine editor, History list and
detail, Settings, and the recovered workout runner. Every surface remains an
**Operate** surface. Corner Companions are static, text-free atmosphere in
reserved whitespace and never become navigation, coaching, status, validation,
or data.

## Audience, job, and action

Guests browse a public exercise catalog. Members browse their catalog, edit an
owned routine, inspect immutable workout history, manage settings, and log a
workout. The existing product action on each route remains primary; the animal
has no action, accessible name, focus target, pointer target, or semantic state.

## Chosen composition

The approved comp-first target is the uncropped Corner Companions board at
`.impeccable/mocks/companion-concepts/corner-companions-board.png`, interpreted
through the passed pilot Library-independent landing, member-home, and Progress
captures. Each rollout surface gets at most one purpose-built full-body
companion in a dedicated heading-side or introductory whitespace slot. Do not
crop the board or use it at runtime.

## Memorable moment

Across the operating journey, a quiet animal vignette appears only when the
layout has spare room. As soon as product content or critical state needs the
space, the vignette disappears without leaving a hole or changing the task.

## Responsive translation

- Phone at 320, 390, and 430 CSS pixels is content-first and may show no art.
- At 820 CSS pixels, a companion appears only after measured reserved space and
  non-overlap prove it safe.
- At 1,280 and 1,440 CSS pixels, art may occupy the dedicated heading-side slot.
- True native 200% browser zoom is separate from DevTools Protocol page scale;
  Library and History join the headed native-zoom record.
- Forced colors removes the artwork. Reduced motion remains fully static.
- Runner art appears only in a recovered, online, neutral overview/header state
  and collapses for active logging, timer, guidance, recovery, error, offline,
  reconciliation, retry, and terminal states.

## Asset and semantic constraints

Use original transparent WebP companions with exact prompts, SHA-256 hashes,
and same-basename provenance sidecars. A genuinely public Library asset is the
only rollout asset eligible for the public cache. Editor, History, Settings,
and runner assets are owned-only. Every image uses `alt=""`, `aria-hidden`,
`draggable="false"`, `pointer-events: none`, failure collapse, explicit
dimensions, and the shared `DecorativeCompanion` registry and tokens.

## Protected product regions

Never overlap or compete with Library search/filter/create controls; editor
topology, inputs, validation, save/retry, and destructive review; History
filters, rows, pagination, immutable data, and detail records; Settings
verification, inputs, save/error, account, and deletion controls; runner
identity, progress, notices, guidance, timer, logging, retry, terminal state,
or fixed navigation.

- If Settings or runner cannot retain a safe noncritical slot, record the exact
